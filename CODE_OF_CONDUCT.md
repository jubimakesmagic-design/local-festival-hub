# Code of Conduct

Local Festival Hub aims to be a respectful, practical, and welcoming open-source project.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and constructive.
- Assume good intent while staying clear about problems.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.
- Respect different experience levels and local contexts.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or conduct
- Publishing private information without permission
- Spam, trolling, or repeated off-topic disruption

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments and may block participants who violate this code of conduct.
